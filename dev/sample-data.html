Sample data

A development tool, not part of the app. It fills the same database the app uses with five weeks of invented training so the charts and screens have something to show.

!Everything this writes is fake. Erase it before you log anything real — a mix of the two is worse than either.
Reading the database…

Loading always clears what is there first. Sessions are keyed by an auto-incrementing id, so loading twice on top of itself would silently give you every session twice.

Then open the app. Sample data lands on the five weeks up to today, so the rotation, the block review and every chart have real history behind them.

What it creates

25 sessions across the full A–F rotation with every set logged, 35 daily weigh-ins trending up about 0.45 kg a week, five weeks of tape measurements, two elbow niggles, five physique check-in entries and one form-check reference. Loads come from the plan's own seed maxes and climb about 1.2% a week, so the working-max review has something to propose.
